Privacy Policy / Política de Privacidade

Last updated: November 24, 2026.

1. YouTube API Services

DexINF uses the YouTube API Services. By using DexINF you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

O DexINF usa os Serviços de API do YouTube. Ao usá-lo, você concorda com os Termos de Serviço do YouTube e com a Política de Privacidade do Google (links acima).

2. What data we access

  • When the owner connects a channel, Google returns an OAuth token with the scopes youtube.upload (upload videos, thumbnails and metadata) and youtube.readonly (read the connected channel's name and ID).
  • We read only the connected channel's ID and title, and the IDs of videos uploaded by DexINF.

3. How we use and store it

  • The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel.
  • The OAuth token, channel ID/title and uploaded video IDs are stored only on the owner's computer. DexINF has no server and sends this data to no one except Google's YouTube API.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking, and we do not collect data from viewers or any third party.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use YouTube data for advertising or for training models.

5. Deletion and revoking access

  • You can revoke DexINF's access at any time at the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Stored API data is deleted when access is revoked or when the channel is disconnected, and in any case API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • To request deletion, contact us at the email below and we will delete it within 7 days.
